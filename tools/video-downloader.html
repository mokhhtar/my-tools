How it works

1
Paste a URL Paste any video URL and click Inspect to preview the video and see all available quality options.
2
Choose format & quality Select Video (MP4) or Audio only (MP3, FLAC, WAV…) and pick a resolution.
3
Download with real-time progress A live progress bar shows speed, ETA, and downloaded size. Files are saved to the downloads/ folder automatically.
4
Open your downloads folder Click Open downloads folder to find your files instantly.
